01

The island in 60 seconds

Hudayriyat is a 30 sq km island directly opposite Al Bateen — 11 km, about ten minutes, from Hamdan Street. Abu Dhabi earmarked it for housing in Plan 2030 (2009) and built a US$50M bridge to it in 2012. Then nothing happened for six years: the “bridge to nowhere”.

The island opened as a public beach in May 2018 and as the Leisure & Entertainment District (Marsana, 321 Sports, OCR Park, Circuit X, Bab Al Nojoum) in November 2020, under Modon Properties — a government developer created in January 2018. On 13 June 2023 Modon revealed a 51 million sqm master plan, 53.8% the size of Abu Dhabi Island (the extra land is reclamation): two man-made residential hills, 53.5 km of coastline, Surf Abu Dhabi and a UCI velodrome as anchors.

Residential sales began 15 March 2024. In 30 months Modon launched eleven phases across nine communities, disclosed AED 24.75B+ from just five of them, and made Hudayriyat Abu Dhabi’s #1 district by value. Every home is off-plan; none is built.

The one-liner to memorise: the hottest launch market in Abu Dhabi, cheaper per sqft than Saadiyat, with a government developer behind it — and a market that has never yet handed over a key, rented a unit, or proven a resale premium. An expert says both halves.

The pattern
Leisure gets built. Headline anchors slip 6–18 months. No home has been handed over yet.
Modon’s off-plan record on this island is untested. Its balance sheet is not the worry — H1 2026 profit AED 2.2B, cash AED 8.6B, backlog AED 65.4B. Precedent to know: Modon’s Reem Hills had delivered zero homes 19 months past its Jan 2025 dates ⚠.

The one-bridge problem

About 4,000+ sold homes and plots hand over 2027–2030 onto a single six-lane bridge from Al Bateen. Second access is only at tender: two DMT bridges (bids Jan–Feb 2026, no award found) and twin underwater tunnels + a 4.8 km highway to Mussafah (PPP advisory tender, Jun 2026). Bus 73 runs Reem–Hudayriyat. No ferry, no rail. Sell the tunnels as intent with no date.

Sell it right

Lead with the government developer
“The island is owned and built by Modon — 84.76% held by the Abu Dhabi Government since October 2025, AED 65B revenue backlog, AED 8.6B cash.” Verifiable on ADX. The delivery question then becomes about schedule, not survival.
Sell the payment plan and the dated sell-outs
40/60 on the hills, 50/50 on Village and Bashayer, 5% to reserve at Golf Estates. Then the record: Village same day, Wadeem 72 hours, Bashayer one day, Golf Estates AED 13B+ in days. All Modon-stated, all dated.
Budget the slip out loud
“Al Naseem moved from Q4 2026 to Q4 2027. Plan for 2028.” Volunteering it converts the sceptic and survives the SPA stage. Then point to what did open: Surf, tennis, Marsana.
Know the protections cold
ADREC project escrow · 2% transfer fee vs Dubai’s 4% · freehold for all nationalities · Golden Visa at AED 2M · Modon–ADIB up to 75% off-plan financing for future launches (15% during build, 5–10% at handover).
Match the buyer
Hudayriyat = end-user lifestyle (surf, cycling, ten minutes from Al Bateen) + early-cycle entry below Saadiyat prices. A yield client belongs on Reem (6–8% apartments). A “flip before handover” client needs to hear 2.67% and 192 days.
Quote prices with their pedigree
“Modon’s page says from 6.6M” is a fact. “Nawayef West launched at 6M” is a broker sentence Modon never wrote. The register shows 225 Al Naseem villas at exactly 7,824,194 — that is what a real launch price looks like.

Never say

  • “30–50% appreciation since launch.” The register says +2.67% median; the like-for-like series says +7.5% in 22 months.
  • “6–8% rental yield.” Nothing is built; there are zero leases. Any yield is a projection.
  • “Handover next year.” The earliest key on the island is Q4 2027 and it has moved once already.
  • “Modon launched Nawayef West at 6M / Wadeem at 3.2M.” Modon never printed either number.
  • “Marsana residences” / “Anantara Hudayriyat” / “30,000 residents.” None is in a Modon source.
  • “Ten minutes to the Corniche.” Modon’s own claim is 11 km / ~10 min to Hamdan Street, 15–20 min to the centre — one bridge, at rush hour.
