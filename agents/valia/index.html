03 Selling Points — in order▼

  1. The value math, shown openly. Creek Harbour resales trade ~2,401–2,445/sqft; Valia enters ~6–8% above — the lowest launch premium of 11 recent launches there (typical ~17%). Show the client both numbers; it does the selling.
  2. The district story, dated honestly. Dubai Square Mall (2.6M sqm, under construction, press target ~2028) across the road; Blue Line metro 20% built with a government target of Sep 2029 — both arrive around/before keys. Sell “the doorstep of the mall district”, never a hard date.
  3. Emaar delivery certainty: 118,400+ homes delivered; AED 164.9bn backlog; and at Creek Harbour specifically, the last 12 towers finished ~8 months early on median. Invite verification on Dubai REST (project 4448).
  4. The payment plan: 10% to book, dated instalments to Sep 2029, then a 15-month breather before the final 20% at keys — the developer finances the wait, interest-free.
  5. Real scarcity where it exists: 32 three-beds and 6 four-beds in a 491-home tower. The 1BRs are NOT scarce (245 of them) — sell those on entry price and rental liquidity instead.
  6. Qualify hard: long-hold end-users and income investors. At launch pricing today’s rents give ~4.9–5% gross (~4.1–4.2% net) — never quote ready-stock 6–7%. Not a flip: Emaar requires ~40% paid to assign, and 2024-vintage Creek Harbour launches resell flat. A buyer needing 2028 keys should look at Emaar’s own Valo/Silva — say so, keep the trust.
