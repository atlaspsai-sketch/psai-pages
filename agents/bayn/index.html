03 Selling Points — in order▼

  1. The cheapest door into a true-beach masterplan. Core Villas at ~4.9M sit inside the same gates as 21.8M+ beachfront plots — same beach, marina and enclave life at a fraction of the ticket, and ~25–45% under Emaar’s Oasis per sqft (which has no sea). The discount exists BECAUSE Ghantoot is unproven and ORA is new to the UAE — say it; it filters in the right buyer.
  2. Backing you can verify. Sawiris’ $5.6bn, land from government-owned Modon, AED 1.9bn contractor on site, ADREC escrow. Invite the client to check the project on DARI.
  3. Demand on record. AED 2.7bn sold in year one per ADREC’s market report; phase 1 sold out; 40% Emirati buyers — locals bought first, which international buyers read as signal.
  4. Abu Dhabi economics: 2% transfer fee saves AED 98k vs Dubai on a 4.9M villa; every ticket clears the AED 2M visa threshold (visa timing caveat — completed-property rule; verify per buyer).
  5. The “between” thesis: Dubai Marina under 30 min, Abu Dhabi 44, DWC 27 — and when the 2030 HSR opens, both airports are one stop away from stations ~30 min out. Sell corridors, not a station that doesn’t exist.
  6. Qualify hard: second-home / long-hold capital buyers. Nothing exists on site until Dec 2028; underwrite rent at Saadiyat-like 4.5–5.5% gross at maturity, not Yas/Marjan rates; 2029 exit competes with Fahid Island, Palm Jebel Ali and Siniya supply. A buyer who needs certainty buys Al Jurf (partly delivered) — say so.
