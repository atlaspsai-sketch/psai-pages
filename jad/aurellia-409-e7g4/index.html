5The price against the market, running costs and protections

Price against registered sales

Unit 409 works out at AED 1,204 per sq ft. In our Dubai Land Department database there are 541 registered off-plan two-bedroom sales in Dubai Sports City between October 2025 and September 2026, with a median of AED 1,350 per sq ft. Unit 409 is about 11% below that median.

This compares the offer price with prices already registered in the same district over the past twelve months. It is a comparison, not a forecast.

Service charge

The developer's anticipated service charge is AED 15 per sq ft a year: about AED 18,928, or £3,896, a year from handover. The actual figure is set by the owners' association once the building is running and may differ.

How your money is protected

Every payment goes into the project's escrow account, which is regulated by RERA, Dubai's real estate regulator. The developer draws on it only as construction progresses.

The purchase is registered with the Dubai Land Department in your name (an Oqood registration) once the deposit and fee are paid, so the apartment is recorded as yours from the start, not at handover.

Jad will send the escrow account details with the offer. Pay only into that account, never anywhere else.
